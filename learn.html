Deaf or hard of hearing?

Dial 711 for the free national relay service. An operator will connect you to us at 844-534-4678, or help you dial into the class. There is never a charge for using 711.

Live captions run on screen during every online class. Once you have joined, click the CC button at the bottom of the screen to turn them on and set your own text size.

Would you rather we walked you through it?

Call us at 844-534-4678 — TTY users dial 711 — any weekday before the class. We will stay on the line with you until you are connected. There is no charge and we are happy to do it.
